WindACT is a 36-month project (2026–2029) funded by the Sustainable Energy Authority of Ireland (SEAI). It is based at the Galway Sound Lab, University of Galway.

**Central question:** when does annoyance from wind turbine sound become a legally unreasonable nuisance?

## Work packages

| WP | Title | In plain words |
|---|---|---|
| WP1 | Project management | Running the project, the team and the Steering Group. |
| WP2 | Theory and policy context | How Irish law and policy treat wind turbine noise and nuisance. |
| WP3 | Wind turbine sound features and sound quality | Measuring sound near wind farms and surveying residents. |
| WP4 | Effects on psychological variables | Lab listening experiments on what makes turbine sound annoying. |
| WP5 | Dissemination and impact | Sharing results with communities, industry, policy makers and researchers. |
