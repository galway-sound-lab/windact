We want to hear from people who live near wind farms, as well as from industry, planners and policy makers.

## Community briefings

We will hold online briefings on our findings for communities and stakeholders. Dates will be posted here.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who funds the project?**
The Sustainable Energy Authority of Ireland (SEAI). The research team is independent, and results will be published openly whatever they show.

**Will my personal information be shared?**
No. Everything we publish is anonymised. Participation is voluntary and covered by University of Galway ethics approval.
