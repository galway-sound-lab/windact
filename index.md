# Wind turbine noise, annoyance and the law

<p class="lead">WindACT studies when the sound of wind turbines becomes annoying for people living nearby, and when that annoyance might count as a nuisance under Irish law. The project brings together acoustics, psychology and law.</p>

<div class="cards">
  <div class="card"><h3>Listen</h3><p>Months of sound measurements near Irish wind farms.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><h3>Ask</h3><p>A survey of residents about how they experience turbine sound.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><h3>Test</h3><p>Listening experiments on which sound features drive annoyance.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><h3>Inform</h3><p>Evidence for fair planning, regulation and community dialogue.</p></div>
</div>
