We follow the FAIR principles and the University of Galway Research Data Management Policy.

**What we share:** anonymised data only, after each project phase is complete. This includes acoustic indicators, aggregated survey responses and experiment results, together with a data dictionary.

**What we do not share:** anything that could identify a person or a home. This includes raw audio, precise locations and survey records that could be linked to individuals.

Datasets are deposited on Zenodo (or the University repository) with a DOI, not stored in this GitHub repository.
